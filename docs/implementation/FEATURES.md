## Exact text and publication recovery corrections

Exact Markdown/HTML reads, native save/patch/delivery and text edits preserve a leading U+FEFF scalar and astral characters. Public byte comparisons retain the previous revision and reject invalid surrogates. Local remote-publication preflight failures now carry explicit invocation-only non-dispatch evidence; fresh editor saves can release the attempt and save a corrected draft. Dispatched and earlier uncertain requests keep their reconciliation rules. Receiver-dependent publisher callbacks retain their client. Focused regressions and the expanded isolated document consumer exercise these behaviors.
